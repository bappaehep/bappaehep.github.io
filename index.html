01 · ABOUT

Understanding the matter created in heavy-ion collisions.

I am a final-year PhD student at the National Institute of Science Education and Research (NISER), India, and a member of the STAR Collaboration at RHIC, Brookhaven National Laboratory.

My research combines experimental high-energy physics, large-scale data analysis, physics simulations, phenomenological studies, and Bayesian inference. My doctoral work addresses two complementary questions: how fluctuations can reveal the structure of the QCD phase diagram, and how radial-flow fluctuations can illuminate the dynamics of the quark–gluon plasma.
